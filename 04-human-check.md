# Step 4 — Human Check: My Decisions

**Input:** The draft guide and uncertainty list from Step 3.

**Action:** Show me the draft and stop. Ask me which points to keep, change, or cut; which concepts matter in my class; whether examples match what the instructor taught; and what I can explain in my own words. Record my responses as my decisions. If I have not checked the actual source or working file, retain the uncertainty label.

**Output:** My keep/change/cut decisions and an approved or corrected guide.

**Good looks like:** I can identify inaccurate examples, select what to study, and explain at least one important connection myself.

**Check:** Do not move to Step 5 until I answer. Never invent my approval, my understanding, or peer feedback.
