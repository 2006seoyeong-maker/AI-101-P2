# Step 3 — Draft the Guide

**Input:** The source checklist and gap questions from Steps 1–2.

**Action:** Make a compact concept–example–connection guide. Use examples actually visible in the supplied material or my work. Explain why each example illustrates the concept. Separate confirmed facts, reasonable suggestions, and unknowns. Include the diagnostic questions, but leave their answers hidden for my attempt.

**Output:** A draft study guide and a list of uncertain points for my review.

**Good looks like:** The guide helps me understand why a concept relates to an example and makes it easy to spot gaps across the whole supplied document.

**Check:** Trace every course-specific statement to the supplied page, note, or work sample. Remove or label unsupported claims.
