# Start Here — Post-Class Review Workflow

## Goal

Turn one set of class slides, my in-class keywords or notes, and any relevant project brief into a study guide that helps me understand concepts, examples, and their connections. The guide should help me discover weaknesses I did not already notice.

## Inputs

- A specific lecture's slides or a project brief that I am permitted to use.
- My own keywords, notes, work sample, and description of what confused me.
- `process-map.canvas` and the step files in this folder.

## Directions

Follow `01-gather.md` through `05-recall.md` in order, one step at a time. State the input used and the output made after each step. Do not assume that a screenshot includes the full assignment. Use only the supplied class material for course-specific claims. Label any missing or uncertain explanation as a question. Include the slide or page number when available.

At Step 4, stop and show me the draft. Ask me what to keep, change, or remove. Do not continue until I respond. I decide what matters for my class and how I understand it in my own words.

## Final output and checks

Produce a guide with (1) a short list of all major sections or requirements in the provided material, (2) concept–example–connection notes, (3) uncertain points, and (4) review questions for every major section, including sections I initially felt confident about. Make clear what I confirmed and what remains unverified. After my recall attempt, add my corrections; do not answer the recall questions before I try.
