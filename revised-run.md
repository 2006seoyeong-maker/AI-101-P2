## Human check

The student confirmed the description of their work and the uncertainty about the mask. The student's main difficulty was making the two type roles look different and managing the layers used for the airline mood board.

## Concept → example → connection

| Concept | Evidence in the student's work | What to check or try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typography hierarchy | “fly beyond” is much larger than the small tagline. Primary and secondary type are both labeled as Futura variants. | Identify the exact role of each type style. ## Review questions

1. Without looking at the board, explain what makes “fly beyond” the main message and what role the tagline plays.
2. Which specific visible property separates your primary and secondary type styles?
3. What is the difference between deleting image pixels and hiding them with a mask?
4. What change occurs when you hide the Hue/Saturation layer? What stays editable in the smart object?
5. Can you point to three actual non-destructive edits in the final Photoshop file and explain what each does?
