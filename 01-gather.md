# Step 1 — Gather and Scope

**Input:** One lecture's slides or project brief, my keywords or notes, and any related work sample.

**Action:** Identify the document title, pages available, and my stated difficulty. List every major section or requirement visible in the supplied material. Mark missing pages or missing notes. Do not fill those gaps from general knowledge.

**Output:** A source list and a coverage checklist for the supplied material.

**Good looks like:** The checklist includes topics I did not already name as weak, and each item points to a page, slide, or supplied note where possible.

**Check:** Compare the checklist against the material supplied. If only an excerpt is available, label it an excerpt.
