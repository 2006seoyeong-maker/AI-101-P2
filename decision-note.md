# Decision Note — First Run and Revision

Date: October 5, 2026

## Intent

Use the student's Digital Communication mood board to connect typography hierarchy and non-destructive Photoshop editing to examples in their own work. The guide should help the student explain the concepts rather than simply provide a finished design decision.

## Gap found

The first run identified a white layer mask and explained what masks can do. The screenshot did not show that the mask had actually hidden part of the image. The first run also named the primary and secondary Futura variants without giving the student a concrete way to evaluate their visible difference.

## Change made and why

The revised run distinguishes a technique being present in the Layers panel from being demonstrated in the final visual work. It asks the student to check the mask's actual effect in Photoshop and offers size, weight, spacing, and placement as ways to make the two type roles clearer. It adds questions that require the student to explain the result from memory.

## Evidence and remaining check

The student confirmed the interpretation of the screenshots in conversation. The actual Photoshop file has not been inspected here, so the mask effect and final three demonstrated techniques still need checking.

## Peer feedback and second revision

The student reported a friend's feedback: reviewing the things they know they did not learn is useful, but focusing only on self-identified weaknesses can miss other weak areas. This exposed a coverage gap in the workflow. The revised guide now begins with a brief check across all sections and requirements, including topics the student initially feels confident about. The student then chooses which answers need deeper review. The extra check is a proposed change to test; its effectiveness has not yet been measured.
