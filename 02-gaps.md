# Step 2 — Find Known and Hidden Gaps

**Input:** Step 1's coverage checklist and my notes or work sample.

**Action:** Record the concepts I said I missed. For every checklist section, also create one short question I should be able to explain, even if I think I know that section. Distinguish evidence in my work from a proposed interpretation. Do not treat the mere presence of a Photoshop layer as proof of a visible editing effect.

**Output:** A list of known gaps, possible hidden gaps, and one diagnostic question per section.

**Good looks like:** The questions can reveal misunderstandings outside my self-identified weak areas.

**Check:** Ensure every section in the supplied material has a question; flag any answer that the source does not establish.
