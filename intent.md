# Project 2 — Intent (Draft 1)

## My problem

The pace of my lectures is fast, so I often miss key concepts and examples. How can I review and organize what I missed after class while still deciding for myself what matters and how I understand it?

## My current process

I save the lecture slides and write down keywords during class. Later, I look through the slides again. I tend to lose track when I try to understand how the concepts connect and why the instructor explains them that way.

## What a useful result would look like

A study guide that shows the concepts, examples, and connections at a glance, along with review questions, would help me study.

## What I must decide myself

I will decide which ideas are important and explain in my own words how I understand them. I will not let AI make those decisions for me.

## My current process map (draft)

Receive slides → write keywords during class → review slides later → identify concepts and explanations I did not understand.
