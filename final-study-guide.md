# Digital Communication Review Guide — Airline Mood Board

**Workflow run:** October 5, 2026  
**Material reviewed:** DIGI 130 Project 1 brief, visible pages 17–18 of 35; screenshots of the student's airline mood board and Photoshop Layers panel; the student's explanations in conversation. This guide covers only the supplied excerpt and work samples.

## Coverage check

The visible brief asks for two logo inspirations, two literal images and keywords, primary and secondary color swatches with HEX values, typography hierarchy, a pattern, a business card mockup, and screenshots showing at least three non-destructive Photoshop techniques. The student's mood board shows examples in these categories. The full 35-page brief was not supplied.

## Recall attempt and feedback

1. **Type roles:** The student said changing type style, weight, and color creates a visual difference. **Feedback:** Correct; size and spacing are also possible. A different type family is optional if the two roles are clear.
2. **Three techniques:** The student named the three marked techniques and said the smart object's effect was not confirmed. **Feedback:** Correct to distinguish the icon's presence from proof of how it was used. The white mask's visual effect is also not confirmed by the screenshot.
3. **HEX codes:** The student said they provide accurate information. **Feedback:** Correct; they allow exact reuse of the colors.
4. **Logo and illustration roles:** The student called the logos the original direction and the illustrations the result. **Feedback:** The logos are references for direction. In this mood board, the illustrations establish theme and mood; whether they are final assets needs a separate decision.
5. **Pattern:** The student said viewers could recognize the brand from the pattern without inspecting it closely. **Feedback:** Correct as a design goal; consistent use of the pattern can support recognition.

## Revisit list

- Decide which properties make the primary and secondary type roles visibly distinct in the actual board.
- Open the Photoshop file and demonstrate the effect of each of the three non-destructive techniques, especially the smart object and mask.
- Decide whether the two airplane illustrations are references, proposed assets, or final assets, and label them accordingly.
- Compare this excerpt-based checklist with the remaining pages of the project brief before claiming the whole assignment is covered.

## Workflow revision informed by peer feedback

The student's peer warned that reviewing only known weak areas can miss other gaps. This run therefore asked questions about color codes, imagery, and pattern as well as the student's stated difficulties with type and layers. The imagery question revealed a distinction worth revisiting: inspiration on a mood board versus a final asset.
