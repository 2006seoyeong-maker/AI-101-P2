# Step 5 — Recall and Correct

**Input:** The guide after my Step 4 decisions and its diagnostic questions.

**Action:** Present one question at a time across all major sections, including topics I initially thought I knew. Let me answer from memory before giving feedback. Compare my answer with the provided class material, identify a mismatch or uncertainty, and invite my correction. Summarize which sections I explained, which remain uncertain, and which to revisit later.

**Output:** The final study guide, my attempted answers, corrections, and a short revisit list.

**Good looks like:** The record shows my own explanation and discovers any weak areas that the initial self-assessment missed.

**Check:** Do not mark understanding based only on rereading or recognizing a prepared answer. If the supplied pages are incomplete, do not claim whole-course coverage.
