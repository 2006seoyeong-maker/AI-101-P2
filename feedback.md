# Peer Feedback — Project 2

Date reported: October 5, 2026

## Feedback received

The student reported that a friend thought the guide was useful for reviewing things the student had not learned. The friend also noted that reviewing only the parts the student already considers weak may miss weaknesses in other parts of the material.

## Decision and change

Keep the focused review of known difficulties. Add a short coverage check across all major sections of the provided class material before choosing what to study deeply. Ask one question per section, including sections that initially seem familiar. The student decides which answers reveal a gap and what to revisit.

## How to check the change

On a later lecture or project brief, compare the initial list of weak topics with the topics uncovered by the coverage questions. Record any overlooked weak area and whether the revised guide helped identify it.
